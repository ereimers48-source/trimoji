Returns & Refund Policy

Every Trimoji item is custom-printed just for you, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, wrong size ordered, or personal preference. We do want you to love what you get, so if something isn't right, we'll make it right.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, defective, or isn't what you ordered, email us at hello@trimojiapparel.com within 30 days of delivery with your order reference and a clear photo of the problem. We'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund, your choice. You don't need to send the item back.

Order changes and cancellations

Orders go into production quickly. If you need to change your shipping address or cancel, email us as soon as possible. We can only do this if your order hasn't started production.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking shows delivered but you didn't receive it, or the package is lost in transit, contact us and we'll look into it with the carrier and reship or refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. They typically appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Shipping address

Orders ship to the address you enter at checkout. We can't cover orders returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, but we'll help you arrange a reshipment.
